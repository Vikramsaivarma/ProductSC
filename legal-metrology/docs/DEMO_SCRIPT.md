# DEMO_SCRIPT.md — 3-minute walkthrough

**Presenter**: project owner. **Audience**: SIH judges / evaluators.

## 00:00–00:15 — Problem & hook
> Every packaged product sold in India must carry mandated declarations — MRP,
> manufacturer, net quantity, date of manufacture. Enforcement officers at Legal
> Metrology departments eyeball thousands of labels a day. Today we turn that
> into an AI-powered inspection that's faster, consistent, and audit-ready.

## 00:15–00:45 — Upload flow
- Live on the `/upload` screen.
- Drag in 2–3 photos of a packaged good (e.g. a bag of chips).
- Click Analyze. Show the progress feedback.

## 00:45–01:20 — Report generation
- Land on `/reports/[id]`.
- Point to: compliance score gauge (e.g. 21/100), the declarations table
  (what Gemini extracted), and the font-size analysis.
- Scroll to the violations — show a rule `LM-01` with its actual vs expected.

## 01:20–01:45 — Download PDF
- Click "Download PDF" and open the government-styled report cover + violations.

## 01:45–02:20 — Dashboard
- Go to `/dashboard`. Show KPI cards (total scanned, compliance rate, critical
  violations) and the charts: scan trend, violations by category, top violated
  rules. Emphasize it's driven by **real scanned products**, not fake data.

## 02:20–02:45 — Search
- `/search` → type a brand or product name → results with status badges.

## 02:45–03:00 — Closing
> AI extraction + a deterministic Legal Metrology rule engine → instant,
> standardized, PDF-ready inspection reports for enforcement teams. Built for
> the real rules officers work with every day.
